# A1 Fundament: From "I Know This" to "I Can Say It"

You already know most of A1. You'd recognise these words and structures in a text. But when you have to **say** something, you pause, translate in your head, and lose the verb. **Fundament** fixes exactly that. It is not a beginner course. It is five short units that turn fragile knowledge into fast, reliable production, so A2 and B1 can be built on solid ground.

---

## What you'll be able to do at the end of A1

- Introduce yourself and answer personal questions **within about 3 seconds**, and ask good follow-up questions.
- Buy food and everyday things; ask for prices and quantities.
- Describe your day and agree on a time.
- Make requests, ask for help, understand simple rules.
- Ask for and give directions; buy a ticket.
- Build German sentences on the [Sentence Map](../Resources/Sentence_Map.md) without thinking about it: verb in position 2, the bracket, questions.

## How an A1 unit works

Every A1 unit is **one file** with the same structure:

1. **🎯 Ziele:** what you'll be able to do.
2. **⚡ Schnelltest (10 min):** a short test, done *before* you study the unit. It shows how much of the unit you need today.
3. **Routing table:** your Schnelltest score (and your [diagnostic](../00_Curriculum/09_Diagnostic_Test.md) category) tells you what to do in each section:

   | Category | What you do in the section |
   |---|---|
   | ⚪ **Skip** | Skip it. |
   | 🟢 **Brief review** | Read the *Refresh* box and do the **timed retrieval** activity. |
   | 🟡 **Consolidate** | Do the whole section. |
   | 🟠 **Intensive** / 🔴 **Repair** | Do the whole section **plus** the ➕ Extra round. Repeat the timed retrieval on two more days. |

   If the Schnelltest and the diagnostic disagree, take the **more cautious** category.
4. **Sections §A, §B, §C …:** one topic each (e.g. `A1-U01-§B` = verb in position 2). Each has:
   - a **Refresh** explanation
   - examples
   - practice
   - a ⏱️ **timed retrieval** activity
   - a ➕ **Extra** round
5. **Everyone does these, whatever the routing:**
   - 🗣️ the **fluency and speaking tasks** at the end of the unit (the real goal of A1)
   - 👄 the pronunciation minute
   - the self-check
   - the flashcards

**Why the speaking tasks are for everyone:** even if you know every form, A1 is about *speed*. A section you "skip" still comes back in the fluency tasks, under time pressure.
