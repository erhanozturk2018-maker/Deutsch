# A1 Checkpoint — The Gate to A2

> **Level:** A1 · **Time:** about 2.5 hours in **two sittings** + 20 min scoring
> **Before this:** all five A1 units, or the Fast track from your [diagnostic](../00_Curriculum/09_Diagnostic_Test.md)
> **This checkpoint decides:** are you ready for A2, or which A1 sections should you strengthen first?
> **Revising first?** The [A1 Recap](A1_Recap/00_Overview.md) has the level's Wortschatz, Redemittel and Grammatik on three sheets.

## What is assessed

The A1 can-do targets of the course. You can:
- introduce yourself and answer simple personal questions **within about 3 seconds**, and ask follow-up questions
- buy food and everyday things; ask for prices and quantities
- describe your day and agree on a time
- make requests, ask for help, understand simple rules
- ask for and give directions; buy a ticket
- understand simple notices, messages, announcements and short conversations
- write short messages on everyday topics

## How it works

- **Two sittings**, ideally on the same day or two days in a row:
  - **Sitting 1** (about 70 min): reading, language in context, writing
  - **Sitting 2** (about 70 min): listening, fluency, speaking
- **No dictionary, no notes, no AI help** while you answer. Mark guesses with `?`; they count as wrong.
- **Answer keys** are at the end of each part. Open them only when the part is finished.
- **Speaking and writing** are rated with the [Rubrics](../Resources/Rubrics.md). Get a second opinion (AI prompt in the rubrics, a tutor or a tandem partner) whenever you can.

**To pass, you need all six:**

| Part | Pass mark |
|---|---|
| 1 Reading | ≥ 9 / 12 (75%) |
| 2 Language in context | ≥ 18 / 24 (75%) |
| 3 Writing | rubric average ≥ 2.5 |
| 4 Listening | ≥ 8 / 10 (75%+) |
| 5 Fluency | ≥ 11 / 20 ✓ ("Developing" or better) |
| 6 Speaking | rubric average ≥ 2.5 |

The pass marks are deliberately demanding. Passing means A2 will build on solid ground, not on things you only recognise.

## Part 1 — Reading (15 min)

### Text A: a notice at the swimming pool

> **Hallenbad Nord – Öffnungszeiten**
> Montag geschlossen · Dienstag bis Freitag 7:00–21:00 Uhr · Samstag und Sonntag 9:00–18:00 Uhr
> Letzter Einlass: eine Stunde vor Schluss.
> **Wichtig:** Kinder unter 10 Jahren dürfen nur mit Erwachsenen ins Wasser. Bitte vor dem Schwimmen duschen! Essen und Trinken nur im Café.

Richtig (true) or falsch (false)?
- **1.1** Man kann am Montag schwimmen.
- **1.2** Am Samstag ist das Bad bis 18 Uhr geöffnet.
- **1.3** Ein Kind mit 8 Jahren darf allein ins Wasser.
- **1.4** Am Freitag kann man um 20:30 Uhr noch ins Bad gehen.

### Text B: an email at work

> Hallo zusammen,
>
> am Freitag hat unsere Kollegin Sandra Geburtstag. Wir wollen eine Überraschung machen: Um 12:30 Uhr gibt es Kuchen in der Küche im 3. Stock. Bitte bringt 5 Euro für das Geschenk mit – Martin sammelt das Geld bis Donnerstag. Sandra darf natürlich nichts wissen! Wer kann einen Kuchen backen? Schreibt mir bitte bis morgen.
>
> Viele Grüße
> Petra

Choose a, b or c.
- **1.5** The party is … a) on Thursday. · b) on Friday at 12:30. · c) on Friday evening.
- **1.6** The colleagues should … a) bring 5 euros. · b) buy a present. · c) call Sandra.
- **1.7** Martin … a) is baking a cake. · b) is collecting the money. · c) has a birthday.
- **1.8** Petra wants to know … a) who can bake a cake. · b) when Sandra comes. · c) where the kitchen is.

### Text C: small ads

> **A** Zimmer frei! 16 m² in netter WG (3 Personen), Nähe Uni, 420 € warm, ab 1. März. Bitte nur Nichtraucher.
> **B** Verkaufe Damenfahrrad, 3 Jahre alt, sehr gut, 120 €. Nur Abholung am Wochenende.
> **C** Deutschkurs A2 am Abend: Dienstag und Donnerstag, 18:30–20:00 Uhr, Start 5. März, Volkshochschule Mitte.
> **D** Suche Babysitter für zwei Kinder (4 und 7 Jahre), Montag und Mittwoch nachmittags, 13 € pro Stunde.
> **E** Yoga für Anfänger – samstags um 10 Uhr im Park, kostenlos, bitte eine Matte mitbringen.

Which ad fits each person? One ad is not needed.
- **1.9** Kemal arbeitet am Tag und möchte abends besser Deutsch lernen.
- **1.10** Laura studiert und sucht ein Zimmer. Sie raucht nicht.
- **1.11** Frau Braun möchte am Wochenende Sport machen, aber sie hat wenig Geld.
- **1.12** Jan sucht ein Fahrrad für seine Freundin.

## Part 2 — Language in context (20 min)

### 2A: Mira's email

Fill each gap. The hint in brackets tells you what's needed.

> Liebe Anna,
>
> danke für deine Nachricht! Ich **(1)** ___ (*sein*) jetzt seit drei Wochen in Leipzig. Meine Wohnung hat zwei Zimmer und einen Balkon, aber es gibt **(2)** ___ (*no*) Aufzug. **(3)** ___ (*my*) Nachbarin ist sehr nett. Sie **(4)** ___ (*sprechen*) auch ein bisschen Englisch. Jeden Morgen **(5)** ___ ich um halb sieben ___ (*aufstehen*) und fahre mit der Straßenbahn **(6)** ___ (*to work*). Die Arbeit fängt **(7)** ___ (*at*) acht Uhr an. Am Wochenende **(8)** ___ ich die Stadt ___ (*wollen* + *ansehen*). **(9)** ___ (*können*) du mich im Mai besuchen? Ich brauche nur noch **(10)** ___ (*a*) Stuhl für Gäste 😊 Letzte Woche **(11)** ___ (*sein*) ich krank, aber jetzt geht es mir gut. **(12)** ___ mich doch mal ___! (*anrufen*, to Anna)
>
> Liebe Grüße
> Mira

### 2B: Sentences

- **13** Start with *Am Montag*: ich / habe / einen Termin
- **14** Write the question (*du*): ___? — *Ich wohne seit einem Jahr hier.*
- **15** Write the price in words: 12,80 €
- **16** Write article + plural: Flasche
- **17** Kennst du den Hausmeister? – Ja, ich kenne ___ gut. (*him*)
- **18** Make negative: Ich habe heute Zeit.
- **19** Order politely: a coffee to take away.
- **20** Translate: *I rarely go to the cinema.*
- **21** Translate (to a friend): *You mustn't forget your passport.* (*der Pass*)
- **22** Make negative: Wir können morgen kommen.
- **23** Translate: *Is the station far?*
- **24** Write the infinitive: *aufgestanden*

## Part 3 — Writing (25 min)

Write with spell-check **off**. Rate each text with the [writing rubric](../Resources/Rubrics.md#writing-rubric) at A1 level (a "3" at A1: all points covered, the reader can understand everything, simple linking with *und, aber, dann*).

**3.1 — Reply to Petra (Text B), 25–35 words, 10 min**
- say that you're coming to the party
- say whether you can bake a cake, and if not, why not
- ask one question (about the present, the time, the place …)

**3.2 — Invite a friend, 40–50 words, 15 min**

Your friend Sam wants to visit you in your town for a weekend. Write:
- when Sam can come
- two things you can do together
- how Sam gets from the station to your flat
- what Sam should bring

<details>

**3.2**
> Hallo Sam! Komm doch am 12. Mai! Wir können zusammen auf den Markt gehen und abends ins Kino. Vom Bahnhof nimmst du die Straßenbahn 4 bis zum Rosenplatz. Dann gehst du links, das Haus ist gleich da. Bring bitte gute Schuhe mit – wir wollen wandern! Bis bald, Alex

</details>

**Score:** the average of the two rubric averages · pass ≥ 2.5

## Part 6 — Speaking (30 min)

### 6A: Your Story Bank A1 recordings (~12 min)

Record **all 8 Story Bank tasks** at A1 level (30 seconds each; requirements in the [Story Bank](../Learner_Workbook/Story_Bank.md)). Think for up to 1 minute before each; no written notes. Save them as `StageA1_Task1` … `StageA1_Task8`.

**Rate Tasks 1, 3 and 7** with the [speaking rubric](../Resources/Rubrics.md#speaking-rubric) at A1 level. Keep the others for your record.

### 6B: Two conversations (~15 min)

Do both situations with an AI examiner (prompt below), a tutor or a tandem partner. Record them.

1. **At the market:** buy what you need for a fruit salad for 4 people. Something isn't available; choose an alternative. Pay.
2. **Making plans:** your friend wants to meet this week. Find a time that works for both of you, and agree on a place.

```text
[A1 SPEAKING EXAM — copy this into your AI app, voice mode if possible]
You are a friendly German examiner. I am taking an A1 speaking test. Speak only German at A1 level, short turns.
Situation 1: you are a market seller (use "Sie"). I want to buy fruit for a fruit salad for 4 people.
One fruit I ask for is sold out; offer an alternative. Give prices. End with the total.
Situation 2: when I say "Situation 2", you are my friend (use "du"). You want to meet me this week.
You are busy on Monday, Tuesday evening and Thursday; free on Wednesday after 18:00 and Saturday afternoon.
Let me find a time and a place.
Do NOT correct me during the situations. After I say "Ende", rate EACH situation separately with these
five criteria, 0–4 each, judged against the A1 level: Task completion · Range · Accuracy · Fluency & coherence ·
Pronunciation (say "cannot judge from text" if you only see a transcript).
Then list my 5 most important errors with corrections.
Start Situation 1: "Guten Tag! Was darf's sein?"
```

**Rate both situations** with the speaking rubric: the AI's rating plus your own. Where they differ by more than 1 point on a criterion, reread the rubric description and decide.

**Score:** the average of the 5 ratings (6A Tasks 1, 3, 7 + 6B situations 1 and 2) · pass ≥ 2.5

### ✅ All six passed

**Congratulations: A1 is solid.** Then:
1. Listen to your Stage 0 and A1 recordings of Tasks 1, 2 and 3 back to back ([Story Bank](../Learner_Workbook/Story_Bank.md#recording-log)) and note what has improved.
2. Start **A2** with [A2-U01 Erlebnisse](../A2/A2-U01_Erlebnisse/00_Overview_und_Wortschatz.md) (the stage overview is on the [A2 page](../A2/README.md)).
3. Keep reviewing your A1 flashcards. A2 will use everything from A1 constantly.

### ✗ One part not passed

Strengthen that part for about a week using the remediation map below, then **retake only that part**. For Part 2, ask an AI for a parallel version, so you don't just remember the answers:

```text
Write 24 new German A1 test items like the ones below, testing exactly the same grammar points
(same sections, same order), with new vocabulary and new sentences. Put the answer key at the end.
[paste the Part 2 items here]
```

### ✗ Two or more parts not passed

That's useful information, not a failure. Spend 2 weeks on the sections the remediation map shows, redo the "For everyone" speaking tasks of the units involved, then retake the parts you missed.
