# Chunk Bank

Your personal collection of **ready-made phrases**: the ones *you* need for *your* life. The lessons give you shared vocabulary; this is where you collect what's missing for your job, your hobbies, your city, your people.

**Where chunks come from:**
- words you missed in [self-talk](../Resources/Speaking_Toolkit.md#self-talk) (*"How do I say …?"*)
- phrases from lesson texts, AI conversations, and your free listening and reading
- corrections from your writing feedback

**Rules:**
1. Collect **whole chunks**, not single words: *einen Termin vereinbaren*, not just *vereinbaren*.
2. Aim for **about 5 new chunks a week**. More is not better, because you won't use them.
3. A chunk only counts as learned when you have **used it yourself** in speaking or writing. Tick the last column then.
4. Put each new chunk into your flashcards too, in the production direction (meaning → German).
