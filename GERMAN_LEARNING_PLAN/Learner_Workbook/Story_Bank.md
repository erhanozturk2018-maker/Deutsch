# Story Bank

Eight speaking tasks that come back again and again: at the start (the [Diagnostic](../00_Curriculum/09_Diagnostic_Test.md)), at the end of A1, at the end of A2, and at the end of B1. Each time the demands grow, and each time you **record** yourself.

Keep all your recordings. The first one might be 20 seconds of short sentences with long pauses; the B1 version should be two fluent minutes with stories, reasons and opinions. **Hearing that difference is the best motivation in the course.**

**How it works:**
- **Stage 0** is recorded in the diagnostic (tasks 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7).
- **Stage A1** is recorded in the [A1 Checkpoint](../A1/A1_Checkpoint.md); stage **A2** in the A2 Exit, and stage **B1** in the B1 Exit.
- Units that practise a task's topic link here and invite you to record an early version.
- **Before recording:** think for up to 1 minute (A1/A2) or 2 minutes (B1). Make **no written notes**; at most 3 key words.
- **Name your files:** `StageA1_Task3`, `StageA2_Task3` …
- **After recording:** rate it with the [speaking rubric](../Resources/Rubrics.md#speaking-rubric), fill in the log below, and listen to your previous stage's version.

## The eight tasks

### Task 1 — Wer bin ich? (Who am I?)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | name, where from, where you live, job or studies, languages, family, one hobby |
| A2 | 60 s | + how long you've lived there (*seit* …), where you grew up and what you did before (Perfekt), why you're learning German (*weil* …) |
| B1 | 2 min | + your life path in a few steps, what you're proud of, plans and wishes (*Ich würde gern …*), something important described with a relative clause |

### Task 2 — Mein Alltag (My daily life)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | a normal weekday with times; at least 3 separable verbs (*aufstehen, einkaufen* …); *immer / oft / manchmal / nie* |
| A2 | 60 s | + what was different yesterday (Perfekt); what you like or don't like about your routine, and why |
| B1 | 2 min | + how your routine used to be (*früher … heute*); what you would change and why (Konjunktiv II); your opinion on stress or work–life balance |

### Task 3 — Ein Erlebnis (An experience)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | last weekend or a trip with *war / hatte* and simple phrases (*Ich war in … Es war super. Das Wetter war …*) |
| A2 | 60 s | a real sequence in Perfekt (*zuerst, dann, danach, am Ende*); with whom; how it was; one small problem |
| B1 | 2–3 min | a full story: background, what happened, a turning point, feelings, how it ended; Präteritum or Perfekt, *als*, *nachdem* |

### Task 4 — Mein Ort (My place)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | your flat or town: *es gibt …*, simple adjectives, what you like |
| A2 | 60 s | + rooms and where things are (*an der Wand, neben dem Fenster*); a comparison with another place (*größer als …*) |
| B1 | 2 min | + advantages and disadvantages of living there, what has changed, whether you'd recommend it and to whom |

### Task 5 — Ein Problem lösen (Solving a problem)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | ask for help in a situation (you're lost, you need something, you don't understand): polite requests with *können*, *Entschuldigung …* |
| A2 | 60 s | explain a problem (something is broken, you're ill, a delay) and ask for a solution; give a reason |
| B1 | 2 min | a complaint or negotiation: describe the problem precisely, express your feelings politely, propose two solutions, react to a counter-proposal |

### Task 6 — Etwas planen (Planning something)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | a voice message: invite a friend (what, when, where), ask them to call back |
| A2 | 60 s | + suggest two options, give a reason for each, react to "I can't" (*Wie wäre es mit …? Hast du Lust …?*) |
| B1 | 2 min | plan an event for a group: weigh options, consider different people's needs, justify your choice, organise tasks |

### Task 7 — Meine Meinung (My opinion)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | *Ich finde … gut / nicht gut / super / langweilig*, plus one simple reason (*Das ist …*) |
| A2 | 60 s | an opinion with *Ich finde, dass …* and two reasons with *weil*; one example |
| B1 | 2 min | a clear position, 2–3 arguments with examples, one counter-argument you answer (*Natürlich … aber …*), a conclusion |

### Task 8 — Ein Mensch, der mir wichtig ist (A person who matters to me)

| Stage | Length | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 30 s | who the person is, age, job, where they live, what they like (*sein / ihr …*) |
| A2 | 60 s | + what you did together (Perfekt), why this person is important to you |
| B1 | 2 min | + personality described with examples, a short story about the two of you, relative clauses (*Sie ist ein Mensch, der …*) |

### After each stage: compare

Listen to your previous and current versions of two tasks back to back and note:
- What can I say now that I couldn't before?
- What was faster or smoother?
- What is still missing, and what is my next step?

## Stufe B2

Ab B2 kommt zu jeder Aufgabe **dieselbe zusätzliche Anforderung**: nicht nur erzählen, sondern **einordnen** — mit Abstufung (*tendenziell, in der Regel*), mit Register-Bewusstsein und mit mindestens zwei Partizipialattributen. Länge: **3 Minuten**, Runde 3 nach dem 4/3/2-Prinzip.

| Task | Zusätzlich auf B2 |
|---|---|
| 1 Wer bin ich? | wodurch Sie geprägt wurden; eine Rollenerwartung und Ihr Umgang damit; Selbstbeschreibung ohne Etikett |
| 2 Mein Alltag | Ihr Alltag als Beispiel für eine allgemeinere Entwicklung — mit Einschränkung |
| 3 Ein Erlebnis | das Erlebnis **und** was es über die Umstände zeigt; Wechsel zwischen Erzählung und Deutung |
| 4 Mein Ort | der Ort als sozialer Raum: wer lebt dort, wer nicht mehr, warum |
| 5 Ein Problem lösen | Verhandlung statt Beschwerde: Angebot, Gegenangebot, Bedingung, Abschluss |
| 6 Etwas planen | Planung mit Zielkonflikt: zwei Interessen, ein Kompromiss, eine Begründung |
| 7 Meine Meinung | These, zwei belegte Argumente, ein eingeräumter Einwand, differenziertes Fazit |
| 8 Ein Mensch | Beschreibung ohne Etikett, mit Beleg statt Behauptung |

Speichern Sie die B2-Aufnahmen als `StageB2_Task<N>` und vergleichen Sie jede mit Ihrer B1-Aufnahme: Was ist **inhaltlich** differenzierter geworden, nicht nur sprachlich?
