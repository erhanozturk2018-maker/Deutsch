# Writing Portfolio

A list of every text you write in the course, with its score and whether you rewrote it. Over months, this shows your writing growing from notes to connected opinion texts. Keep the texts themselves in a folder or document of your choice. Use the [Writing Toolkit](../Resources/Writing_Toolkit.md) loop for each one: plan → write → self-edit → feedback → **rewrite** → log.
