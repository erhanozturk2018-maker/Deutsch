# Listening & Reading: Methods and Sources

Every listening text in this course is written into the lessons themselves, so **you never need an external audio file**. You make the audio with a text-to-speech (TTS) voice. This page explains how. It also gives the listening and reading routines the lessons use, reusable task sheets for any audio or text, and optional outside sources for extra practice.

## How to use text-to-speech

Lesson texts meant for listening are hidden in grey **Transkript** boxes, so that you hear them before you read them.

**Step by step:**
1. Read the **questions** first, so you know what to listen for.
2. Open the Transkript box and **copy the text without reading it**: select it all in one go and copy.
3. Paste it into a tool with a German voice (options below).
4. Listen as the task says, usually twice at most, at normal speed.
5. Answer, then open the transcript and read along while you listen once more.

**Tool options:**

| Tool | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Translate** (web or app) | Choose German as the source language, paste, press the speaker icon | Pressing the speaker again usually plays it more slowly |
| **Microsoft Word** | Paste, set the text language to German, then *Review → Read Aloud* | Speed adjustable; several voices |
| **Microsoft Edge** | Open the course file on a website (e.g. your GitHub repository) and use *Read aloud* | Reads the whole page, so select the section |
| **An AI app in voice mode** | Paste the text: *"Read this German text aloud at natural speed. Don't translate or comment."* | For dialogues: *"Use two different voices or clearly mark the speaker change."* |
| **Phone accessibility** | "Select to speak" (Android) or "Speak selection" (iPhone), with a German voice installed | Useful on the go |

**Tips:**
- **Vary the voice** (male/female, if your tool offers them), so your ear doesn't get used to one speaker.
- For **shadowing**, slow the voice down a little at first.
- If you can't avoid seeing the text while copying, that's OK. Look away and listen before you read in detail.

## The listening routine

Lessons use this sequence. Use it for any audio.

1. **Predict (1 min):** from the title and questions, what will you probably hear? Which words might come up? Say 3–5 of them aloud.
2. **Gist (1st listen):** who is speaking, where, about what? Don't try to catch every word.
3. **Details (2nd listen):** answer the specific questions.
4. **Check with the transcript:** read along while listening. Underline what you missed and ask *why*: a new word? words running together? speed?
5. **Shadow:** speak along with 2–3 key sentences (see [Speaking Toolkit](Speaking_Toolkit.md#shadowing)).

> **Tolerate the unknown.** You will never understand every word, not even at C2. The skill is to catch the important information around the gaps. Practise it on purpose.

## The reading routine

1. **Before reading:** look at the title, pictures (if any), text type (email? ad? forum post?). What do you expect?
2. **Skim:** read fast for the main idea. Don't stop at unknown words.
3. **Scan:** look for the specific information the task asks for.
4. **Guess unknown words:** from context, from word parts (*Kinder**garten**, un**freundlich***), or from English (*Haus, Garten*).
5. **Look up at most 3–5 words**: the ones you need for the task or that come up again and again.
6. **Retell:** close the text and say its main points in 3–5 sentences, aloud. This turns reading into speaking practice.

## Optional sources for extra practice

**None of these is required**: every lesson works on its own. They are good, free, well-known sources for your weekly free listening and reading time. Choose by level and interest; enjoying it matters more than difficulty. Their content changes over time, so no lesson depends on a specific episode.

| Level | Listening / video | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| A1–A2 | Deutsche Welle (DW) *Nicos Weg* (video course, A1–B1) · Easy German (street interviews with subtitles) | DW learning materials · graded readers for learners (e.g. Klett's *Leichte Lektüren* series) |
| A2–B1 | *Slow German* (podcast) · DW *Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten* (slowly spoken news) | *nachrichtenleicht.de* (news in simple German, Deutschlandfunk) |
| B1 | DW *Top-Thema* (short reports with transcripts) · Easy German podcast · *logo!* (children's news, very clear) · *Tagesschau in 100 Sekunden* | DW *Top-Thema* articles · simple newspaper articles on familiar topics |

**How to use them:**
- Use the task sheets above.
- Aim for **"I understand the main points"**, not every word.
- Listen to the same short piece 2–3 times over a week rather than always choosing new ones.
