# Redemittel: Useful Phrases by Function

*Redemittel* are ready-made phrases for doing things with language: greeting, asking, agreeing, complaining, keeping a conversation alive. They are the fastest way to sound fluent. You don't build them word by word; you **retrieve them whole**. Learn them as chunks, say them aloud, and put the ones you need into your flashcards and your [Chunk Bank](../Learner_Workbook/Chunk_Bank.md).

Register tags: `[informal]` = friends, family, people you say *du* to; `[formal]` = *Sie*, official situations; `[spoken]` = typical of speech. No tag = neutral, fine almost everywhere.

|---|---|---|
| Hallo! / Hi! | Hello! / Hi! | informal |
| Guten Morgen! / Guten Tag! / Guten Abend! | Good morning / day / evening | neutral–formal |

## A1: Keeping a conversation going

These turn an interview into a conversation. Use at least one after every answer you get.

| Phrase | Meaning | Register |
|---|---|---|
| Und du? / Und Sie? | And you? | – |
| Wirklich? / Echt? | Really? | *Echt?* informal |
| Seit wann? / Wie lange schon? | Since when? / How long? | – |
| Wie findest du …? | What do you think of …? | informal |
| Warum? | Why? | – |
| Ach so! / Ach, interessant! | Oh, I see! / Oh, interesting! | – |
| Erzähl mal! | Tell me (more)! | informal, spoken |
| Und dann? | And then? | – |
| Was machst du so …? | What do you do (in your free time …)? | informal, spoken |

## A1: When you don't understand

The most useful phrases in the course. Say them without hesitation.

| Phrase | Meaning | Register |
|---|---|---|
| Wie bitte? | Pardon? | – |
| Entschuldigung, ich verstehe das nicht. | Sorry, I don't understand. | – |
| Können Sie das bitte wiederholen? | Could you repeat that, please? | formal |
| Kannst du das noch mal sagen? | Can you say that again? | informal |
| Langsamer, bitte. | More slowly, please. | – |
| Was bedeutet …? | What does … mean? | – |
| Wie sagt man … auf Deutsch? | How do you say … in German? | – |
| Wie schreibt man das? | How do you spell that? | – |
| Moment, … | Just a moment … (to gain time) | spoken |
