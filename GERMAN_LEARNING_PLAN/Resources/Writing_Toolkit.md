# Writing Toolkit

Writing is speaking in slow motion: it gives you time to build sentences you'll later say fast. In this course writing tasks are always **real text types** with a reader and a purpose, never grammar exercises in disguise. This page gives you the writing routine, the self-editing checklist, templates for common text types, and the feedback prompt.

## The writing loop

Every writing task in the course follows five steps:

1. **Plan (2 min):** Who is the reader? *du* or *Sie*? Which content points must be in it? Note 3–5 key words.
2. **Write:** within the time and length given. No translator. If a word is missing, write around it (see [Speaking Toolkit](Speaking_Toolkit.md#talking-around-a-missing-word)) or mark it with `[?]`.
3. **Self-edit** with the checklist below. Read the text once for each point.
4. **Get feedback:** from an AI (prompt below), a tutor or a tandem partner.
5. **Rewrite** the text with the corrections, then log the 1–3 most important mistakes in your [Error Log](../Learner_Workbook/Error_Log.md) and the text in your [Writing Portfolio](../Learner_Workbook/Writing_Portfolio.md).

**Step 5 is where learning happens.** Reading a corrected version is not enough; writing it again is what fixes the pattern.

## Self-editing checklist

Read your text **once for each point**, in this order. The first points catch the mistakes that matter most.

1. [ ] **Verb position:** conjugated verb in position 2 in main clauses; at the end after *weil, dass, wenn* …; second verb parts at the end ([Sentence Map](Sentence_Map.md)).
2. [ ] **Case:** accusative after verbs like *haben, brauchen, kaufen, sehen*; later, dative after *mit, bei, von, zu* …
3. [ ] **Gender:** check every article. When unsure, look the noun up and note it in your flashcards.
4. [ ] **Linking:** are sentences connected (*und, aber, dann, weil, deshalb* …), or is it a list?
5. [ ] **Register:** *du* or *Sie*, used consistently? Greeting and closing right for the reader?
6. [ ] **Spelling and punctuation:** nouns capitalised; comma before *weil, dass, wenn, aber*.

## Getting feedback

Paste this into an AI chat app together with your text. For a full rating with scores, use the rating prompt in [Rubrics](Rubrics.md#ai-rating-prompts).

```text
[WRITING FEEDBACK — copy this into your AI app]
You are a precise, encouraging German teacher. I am at level {A1/A2/B1}.
Task: {paste the task}
My text is below. Please:
1. List my errors (max 8): my version → correct version → category
   (WO word order, VF verb form, CA case, GE gender, AE adjective ending,
   PR preposition, WC word choice, SP spelling, PU punctuation, RE register).
2. Give a corrected version that keeps my ideas and my level. Don't make it more advanced.
3. Show me 2–3 places where a more natural phrase would fit, and give it.
4. Tell me the ONE thing to focus on next time.
My text:
{paste your text}
```

## Openings and closings

| | Informal (*du*) | Semi-formal (*Sie*, someone you know) | Formal (*Sie*, unknown or official) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opening** | *Hallo Sara, / Hi Tom, / Liebe Sara, / Lieber Tom,* | *Liebe Frau Weber, / Lieber Herr Klein,* | *Sehr geehrte Frau Weber, / Sehr geehrter Herr Klein, / Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,* |
| **First line** | *wie geht's dir?* / *danke für deine Nachricht!* | *vielen Dank für Ihre E-Mail.* | *ich schreibe Ihnen, weil …* |
| **Closing** | *Liebe Grüße / Viele Grüße / Bis bald!* | *Viele Grüße / Herzliche Grüße* | *Mit freundlichen Grüßen* |
| **Pronouns** | *du, dich, dir, dein* (lower case) | *Sie, Ihnen, Ihr* (capitalised) | *Sie, Ihnen, Ihr* (capitalised) |

⚠️ After the opening comma, the text starts with a **lower-case** letter (unless the first word is a noun): *Liebe Sara, **w**ie geht's dir?*

## Text type templates

These are skeletons, not texts to copy. Fill them with your own content.

### Note or short message (A1)

> Hallo [Name], ich [what I'm doing]. [Question?] Ich bin um [time] wieder da. Bis später! [your name]

### Personal email (A2)

> Liebe/Lieber [Name],
> danke für [deine Nachricht / deine Einladung]! [Reaction.]
> [Main content: 2–4 sentences, e.g. what you did, what's new.]
> [Question or suggestion: *Hast du am … Zeit? Wir könnten …*]
> Liebe Grüße
> [Name]

### Semi-formal or formal email (A2 → B1)

> Sehr geehrte Frau / Sehr geehrter Herr [Name],
> ich schreibe Ihnen, weil [reason].
> [Details: what, when, where.]
> [Request: *Könnten Sie mir bitte … ?* / *Ich möchte Sie bitten, …*]
> Vielen Dank im Voraus.
> Mit freundlichen Grüßen
> [Full name]

### Forum opinion post (B1)

> [Your opinion in one sentence: *Ich finde, dass …* / *Meiner Meinung nach …*]
> [Reason 1 + example: *Erstens … Zum Beispiel …*]
> [Reason 2: *Außerdem …*]
> [The other side: *Natürlich … aber …* / *Einerseits … andererseits …*]
> [Conclusion: *Deshalb …* / *Insgesamt …*]

More templates (complaint, story, summary, application) are added in A2 and B1 when their units teach them.
