# English → German: Traps for English Speakers

English and German are close relatives. That helps a lot: *Haus, Hand, trinken, kommen*. It also creates traps: words that look the same but mean something else, and English habits that produce wrong German. This page collects the traps that really cause errors, grouped by the stage where they matter. Lessons flag them in their ⚠️ sections and link here.

**How to use it:** don't memorise the list. When a lesson or your [Error Log](../Learner_Workbook/Error_Log.md) shows you've fallen into one of these traps twice, copy that item into your flashcards.

---

## A1

### Word order

| English habit | ❌ Wrong German | ✅ Right German | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Today I work.* | Heute ich arbeite. | Heute **arbeite ich**. | Verb in position 2 ([Sentence Map](Sentence_Map.md)) |
| *Do you work?* | Machst du arbeiten? | **Arbeitest du?** | No "do": move the verb |
| *I am working.* | Ich bin arbeiten. | Ich **arbeite** (gerade). | No continuous tense: the present covers both |
| *I call you tomorrow.* | Ich anrufe dich morgen. | Ich **rufe** dich morgen **an**. | Separable verbs split |
| *I can speak German well.* | Ich kann sprechen Deutsch gut. | Ich kann gut Deutsch **sprechen**. | The second verb goes to the very end |

### Question words that look English

| German | Means | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **wer** | who | ⚠️ not "where" |
| **wo** | where | ⚠️ not "who" |
| **wann** | when (question) | – |
| **wenn** | if / whenever | ⚠️ not a question word (A2) |
| **dann** | then | – |
| **denn** | because; also a question particle | ⚠️ not "then" |

### False friends (A1 words)

| German | Means | ⚠️ Not | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **also** | so, therefore | "also" (= *auch*) | *Also, was machen wir?* — So, what are we doing? |
| **bekommen** | to get, receive | "to become" (= *werden*) | *Ich bekomme ein Paket.* — I'm getting a parcel. |
| **will** (*wollen*) | want(s) | future "will" (= *werden*) | *Ich will nach Hause.* — I want to go home. |
| **fast** | almost | "fast" (= *schnell*) | *Es ist fast acht.* — It's almost eight. |
| **das Handy** | mobile phone | "handy" (= *praktisch*) | *Mein Handy ist leer.* |
| **der Chef / die Chefin** | boss | "chef" (= *Koch/Köchin*) | *Meine Chefin ist nett.* |
| **das Gift** | poison | "gift" (= *das Geschenk*) | – |
| **bald** | soon | "bald" (= *glatzköpfig*) | *Bis bald!* — See you soon! |
| **die Art** | kind, type, way | "art" (= *die Kunst*) | *eine Art Kuchen* — a kind of cake |
| **der Rat** | advice | "rat" (= *die Ratte*) | *Hast du einen Rat für mich?* |
| **der Mist** | rubbish, dung; "damn!" | "mist" (= *der Nebel*) | *Mist! Der Bus ist weg.* [informal] |
| **die Hose** | trousers | "hose" (= *der Schlauch*) | *Die Hose ist zu lang.* |
| **brav** | well-behaved | "brave" (= *mutig*) | *Das Kind ist brav.* |
| **eventuell** | possibly, maybe | "eventually" (= *schließlich*) | *Ich komme eventuell später.* |
| **aktuell** | current | "actually" (= *eigentlich*) | *die aktuellen Nachrichten* |
| **sensibel** | sensitive | "sensible" (= *vernünftig*) | *Er ist sehr sensibel.* |

### Fixed expressions that don't translate word for word

| English | ❌ Calque | ✅ German |
|---|---|---|
| *I'm fine / I'm good.* | Ich bin gut. | **Mir geht's gut.** / **Gut, danke.** |
| *I'm cold.* | Ich bin kalt. | **Mir ist kalt.** (A2 explains why) |
| *I'm hungry.* | – | **Ich habe Hunger.** (more common than *ich bin hungrig*) |
| *I'm right.* | Ich bin richtig. | **Ich habe recht.** |
| *to take a photo* | ein Foto nehmen | ein Foto **machen** |
| *to have breakfast* | Frühstück haben | **frühstücken** |
| *on Monday / at the weekend* | auf Montag / an dem Wochenende | **am** Montag / **am** Wochenende |
| *half past seven* | halb sieben ❌ | **halb acht** (half *to* eight) |
| *I come from Canada.* | Ich komme von Kanada. | Ich komme **aus** Kanada. |
| *What's your name?* | Was ist dein Name? ⚠️ | **Wie heißt du?** (the first is possible, but rare) |
| *twenty-one* | zwanzigeins | **einundzwanzig** (units first) |

### Spelling

- **All nouns are capitalised:** *das Haus, die Idee, das Essen*. So is ***Sie*** (formal *you*) and its forms (*Ihnen, Ihr*).
- ***ich*** is lower case, unlike English *I*.
- ***ß*** (*Straße*) is written *ss* in Switzerland and when typing without German letters: *Strasse*.

## A2

### Talking about the past (A2-U01)

| English | ❌ Calque | ✅ German | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| *I went / I have gone / I was going* | – | Ich **bin gegangen**. | One spoken past tense (Perfekt) covers all three |
| *Did you work yesterday?* | Arbeitest du gestern? (present for past) | **Hast** du gestern **gearbeitet**? | No "do": use the Perfekt |
| *I have worked yesterday.* (impossible in English) | – | Ich **habe gestern** gearbeitet. ✅ | The German Perfekt is fine with *gestern* |
| *I've lived here for three years.* | Ich habe hier drei Jahre gewohnt. | Ich **wohne seit** drei Jahren hier. | Still true → *seit* + **present** |
| *two years ago* | zwei Jahre vor | **vor** zwei Jahren | *vor* comes first |
| *Have you ever been to Rome?* | Bist du jemals in Rom gewesen? | **Warst** du **schon mal** in Rom? | *schon mal* + *war* is the natural form |
| *I've never done that.* | Ich habe nie das gemacht. | Das habe ich **noch nie** gemacht. | *noch nie* = never (up to now) |
| *Have you eaten yet? – Not yet.* | – | Hast du **schon** gegessen? – **Noch nicht.** | *yet* → *schon* / *noch nicht* |
| *I got a present.* | – | Ich **habe** ein Geschenk **bekommen**. | ⚠️ *bekommen* = get, never "become" (= *werden*) |
