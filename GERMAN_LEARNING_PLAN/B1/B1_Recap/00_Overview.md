# B1 Recap — Überblick

> **Level:** B1 · **Stand: B1.1 (Einheiten U01–U06).** B1.2 (U07–U12) is still being written; these sheets grow with it — see [Was noch fehlt](#was-noch-fehlt).
> **What this is:** the level consolidated into three reference sheets. **It answers one question:** *what should I know at this point in B1?*
> **It is not a lesson.** No exercises, no discovery texts. Open it **after** learning something, to check or revise it.

## The three sheets

| Sheet | What is in it | Wann benutzen? |
|---|---|---|
| [01 · Wortschatz](01_Wortschatz.md) | The B1.1 core: biography, people and character, work and applications, health and stress, travel and culture, media and news, plus **abstract nouns**, **collocations**, **verbs with prepositions** and **word formation** | Before a discussion or presentation; when your speech is correct but too simple |
| [02 · Redemittel](02_Redemittel.md) | Language by purpose: narrating, describing and paraphrasing, applying for a job, hypothesising and advising, complaining, comparing cultures, **arguing and weighing up**, explaining a process, steering a conversation | Before a role-play, a discussion or a written task |
| [03 · Grammatik](03_Grammatik.md) | Every B1.1 structure: Präteritum, Plusquamperfekt, *als/wenn/wann*, relative clauses, adjective endings stage 2, n-declension, *zu*-infinitive, *um zu / damit*, da-word + clause, Konjunktiv II in full, genitive, paired connectors, passive | When a form is unclear; before the Midpoint |

## Wie Sie damit arbeiten

1. At B1 the test is no longer "do I know this word" but **"can I use it in a sentence that carries an argument?"** Say full sentences, not single items.
2. Work in **15-minute blocks**, one function or one structure at a time.
3. Whatever you cannot produce, go back to the **unit** named in the sheet and redo that lesson's ⏱️ drill and role-play.
4. Then take the [B1 Midpoint Checkpoint](../B1_Midpoint_Checkpoint.md).

## Was B1 gegenüber A2 wirklich ändert

| | A2 | B1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Sätze** | main clauses + *weil / dass / wenn* | **long sentences**: relative clauses, *nachdem*, *damit*, *obwohl*, two clauses at once |
| **Zeit** | Perfekt for everything spoken | **Präteritum** for narrating, **Plusquamperfekt** for the earlier past |
| **Perspektive** | active only | **passive**, *man*, Konjunktiv II — three ways of not naming the doer |
| **Wortschatz** | concrete, topic-based | **abstract nouns and collocations**: *Entscheidung treffen, Verantwortung übernehmen, Wert legen auf* |
| **Sprechen** | 60 seconds, opinions with *weil* | **2 minutes**: argument, counter-argument, concession, conclusion |
| **Wenn ein Wort fehlt** | you stop | **you paraphrase** — the defining B1 skill |
