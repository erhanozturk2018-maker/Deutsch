# C2 Recap — Überblick

> **Level:** C2 · **Status: Referenz, noch nicht konsolidiert.** The C2 units are not written yet (milestone M11). These three sheets are built from the **CEFR C2 descriptors**; as the units appear, each one is folded in here.
> **It answers one question:** *what should I know at the end of C2?*
> **It is not a lesson** — and at this level it could not be. C2 is reached through volume, real stakes and good feedback. What a reference can do is **name the distinctions** so you notice them in the wild.

## The three sheets

| Sheet | What is in it | Wann benutzen? |
|---|---|---|
| [01 · Wortschatz](01_Wortschatz.md) | Fine distinctions between near-synonyms, idiom sorted by register, metaphor fields, evaluative and rhetorical vocabulary, specialist and literary lexis, productive word formation | When you are choosing between three words that all "work" |
| [02 · Redemittel](02_Redemittel.md) | Nuanced argument, implicature, irony and understatement, rhetorical figures, diplomacy under pressure, mediation, editing another person's German, genre openings and closings | Before writing or speaking where the *effect* matters more than the content |

## Was C2 wirklich verlangt (CEFR, wörtlich zusammengefasst)

- **Verstehen:** understands with ease **virtually everything** heard or read.
- **Zusammenfassen:** can summarise information from different spoken and written sources, **reconstructing arguments and accounts** in a coherent presentation.
- **Sprechen:** expresses themselves spontaneously, **very fluently and precisely**, differentiating finer shades of meaning even in more complex situations.
- **Schreiben:** writes clear, smoothly flowing text in an appropriate style, with a **logical structure that helps the reader notice significant points**.

**In der Praxis heißt das drei Dinge:**

1. **Nuance.** Not "the right word" but *which* right word: *einwenden · widersprechen · bestreiten · in Abrede stellen* are four different moves.
2. **Wirkung.** You choose sentence length, focus and rhythm for effect, the way you choose vocabulary.
3. **Unsichtbare Reparatur.** When something goes wrong mid-sentence, you restructure without anyone noticing. That, not error-freeness, is the C2 fluency criterion.

## Wie Sie damit arbeiten

1. **Lesen Sie mit Stift.** Mark every place where you would have written something different, then ask why the author chose theirs.
2. **Imitieren Sie absichtlich.** One paragraph a week, structure copied exactly, content your own.
3. **Führen Sie ein Nuancenheft**, not a vocabulary list: word, two near-synonyms, what separates them, one authentic sentence.
4. **Lassen Sie redigieren.** At C2 the useful feedback is about **word choice, register and structure** — find a reader who gives that.
5. **Sprechen Sie unter Druck.** Debate, present, interpret. Fluency at C2 is proven only under load.
