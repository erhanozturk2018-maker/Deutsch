# A2 Recap — Überblick

> **Level:** A2 (complete: ten units, three reviews, two checkpoints) · **What this is:** the whole level consolidated into three reference sheets.
> **It answers one question:** *what should I know at the end of A2?*
> **It is not a lesson.** No exercises, no discovery texts — those are in the units. Open this **after** learning, to check or revise.

## The three sheets

| Sheet | What is in it | Wann benutzen? |
|---|---|---|
| [01 · Wortschatz](01_Wortschatz.md) | The A2 core by topic — experiences, people and feelings, home, food, health, clothes, travel, work and phone, plans and festivals, media and tech — plus the A2 verb list with Perfekt forms, verbs with prepositions, adjectives, adverbs and word formation | Before a speaking session; when the same word keeps escaping you |
| [02 · Redemittel](02_Redemittel.md) | Phrases and patterns by purpose: telling a story, reacting, opinions and reasons, restaurant, doctor, shopping and complaints, travel and information, work and telephone, inviting and refusing, advice and wishes, writing an email | Before a role-play, a phone call or a written task |
| [03 · Grammatik](03_Grammatik.md) | Every A2 structure with forms, word order, examples, exceptions and typical errors: Perfekt, dative, prepositions, subordinate clauses, reflexives, modal past, adjective endings, comparison, future, *da*-words, Konjunktiv II chunks, connectors | When a form is unclear; before the Exit Checkpoint |

## Wie Sie damit arbeiten

1. **Cover the German and say it.** At A2 the test is whether it comes out **in a whole sentence**.
2. Work in **10–15 minute blocks**, one topic or one structure at a time.
3. Whatever you cannot produce, go back to the **unit** named in the sheet and redo that lesson's ⏱️ activity — not the whole unit.
4. Put every gap you find into your [Error Log](../../Learner_Workbook/Error_Log.md), not into a new list.
5. Then take the [A2 Exit Checkpoint](../A2_Exit_Checkpoint.md).

## Was A2 gegenüber A1 wirklich ändert

| | A1 | A2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Zeit** | present, *war / hatte* | the **past as a system**: Perfekt for speaking, *war/hatte/konnte* for the rest |
| **Kasus** | nominative, accusative | **+ dative** — verbs, prepositions, indirect objects |
| **Satz** | main clauses, verb in position 2 | **+ subordinate clauses**: *weil, dass, wenn, ob* with the verb at the end |
| **Sprechen** | fixed phrases, 30-second turns | **60-second turns**: reasons, opinions, comparisons, reactions |
| **Ton** | neutral | **politeness**: *Könnten Sie …?, Ich hätte gern …, An deiner Stelle …* |
