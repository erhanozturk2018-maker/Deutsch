# C1 Recap — Überblick

> **Level:** C1 · **Status: Referenz, noch nicht konsolidiert.** The C1 units are not written yet (milestone M10). These three sheets are built from the **CEFR C1 descriptors** and this course's progression; as the units appear, each one is folded in here.
> **It answers one question:** *what should I know at the end of C1?*
> **It is not a lesson.** No exercises. It is a map, a checklist and a precision reference.

## The three sheets

| Sheet | What is in it | Wann benutzen? |
|---|---|---|
| [01 · Wortschatz](01_Wortschatz.md) | Academic and professional lexis, **precise near-synonyms**, connotation, idioms and their register, field-specific language, nominalisation families, the ⬇/neutral/⬆ scale | When your German is right but not *exact*; before writing anything that will be read by specialists |
| [02 · Redemittel](02_Redemittel.md) | Academic writing, hedging, structuring a long argument, diplomatic criticism, moderating, mediating, reviewing, minutes | Before an essay, a meeting, a presentation or a negotiation |
| [03 · Grammatik](03_Grammatik.md) | Nominal style, extended participial constructions, both Konjunktive across registers, the full passive-substitute system, advanced connectors, ellipsis, focus and cohesion | When you must choose between two correct structures for effect |

## Was C1 wirklich verlangt (CEFR, kurz)

- **Hören:** understand extended speech even when it is not clearly structured and relationships are only **implied**.
- **Lesen:** understand long, complex, factual and literary texts, appreciating **distinctions of style**.
- **Sprechen:** express yourself fluently and spontaneously **without much obvious searching**; use language flexibly and effectively for social, academic and professional purposes.
- **Schreiben:** produce clear, well-structured, detailed text on complex subjects, showing **controlled use of organisational patterns, connectors and cohesive devices**.

**In der Praxis heißt das drei Dinge:**

1. **Wahl statt Regel.** At C1 more than one structure is grammatically correct; the level is the **choice** — *Nach Prüfung der Unterlagen* or *Nachdem die Unterlagen geprüft worden waren*, and why.
2. **Konnotation statt Bedeutung.** *sparsam* / *geizig*, *selbstbewusst* / *arrogant*, *entschlossen* / *stur* — same denotation, different verdict.
3. **Kohäsion.** A C1 text holds together by connectors, pronouns, nominalisations and repetition patterns — not by being a list of correct sentences.

## Wie Sie damit arbeiten

1. Take a real German text and mark, in three colours: **connectors**, **nominalisations**, **hedges**. Then write one paragraph imitating it. That single exercise is most of C1.
2. Keep a **Konnotationsheft**: every new word gets a synonym, an antonym, a register mark and one collocation.
3. Record yourself speaking for five minutes on an abstract topic; listen only for **repetition and filler**, and replace them.
4. Write in **named genres** (abstract, review, opinion, minutes) — at C1, genre is half the grade.
